YouTube Habit Tracker — Settings

Tune how videos are classified and what counts as idle. Changes apply to tracking from the moment you save; past time is not re-labelled.

Keywords

Comma- or line-separated. Matched as whole words in the title (×2), channel name (×2) and tags (×1); simple plurals and “-ing” forms match too. YouTube’s own category adds its weight below.

Always-Study / Always-Fun channels

One per line. Use the channel’s display name (e.g. Khan Academy) or its @handle (e.g. @khanacademy). These are added to the built-in lists in config.js. If a channel is in both, Study wins.

YouTube category weights

One Category = weight per line. Positive leans Study, negative leans Fun. Category names are YouTube’s English names.

Idle detection

Chrome reports the computer as idle after this many seconds with no keyboard or mouse input (minimum 15). A locked screen is always Idle.
